VMX Training Courses

Our instructor-led training—available virtually or in the classroom—empowers you to master the VMX server virtualization platform with confidence. Designed for IT professionals and teams, this structured program boosts productivity, lowers operational costs, advances your career, and establishes you as a recognized expert.

Certified instructors from VMX Server Solutions or Authorized VMX Training Partners guide you through VMX concepts and features, from single-node setups to complex clusters, all within a few days.

What You'll Achieve

Training Course Outcomes

01

Deploy & Manage Clusters

Confidently deploy and manage a VMX cluster following industry best practices. Develop a solid understanding of how a VMX cluster operates and plan strategically to minimize downtime.

02

VMs & LXC Containers

Master the key architectural differences between virtual machines (VMs) and LXC containers, and learn to execute seamless live guest migrations.

03

Hyper-Converged Storage

Explore built-in shared storage options within a cluster. Configure VMX and Ceph as a hyper-converged infrastructure (HCI) with optimized Ceph component interactions.

04

High Availability (HA)

Understand how high availability (HA) works for guest systems and learn how to configure it effectively to safeguard business-critical services.

05

Software Defined Networking

Implement Software Defined Networking (SDN) in a clustered environment, enabling centralized management of VLANs, subnets, and security zoning.
